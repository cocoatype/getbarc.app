Barc 25.7 adds support for iOS/watchOS 26 and the new Liquid Glass design. There's also a few small goodies:

- Rendering barcodes has been improved to fill more space in widgets, especially for PDF 417 codes.
- All app icons, both the default and alternates, have seen a bit of a touch-up.
