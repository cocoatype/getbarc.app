Barc 25.8 focuses on the experience of adding barcodes by typing them in manually.

### Barcode Preview

The manual entry form shows you what your barcode will look like before you save it like the other barcode entry methods. You can use this to make sure that your barcode works the way you expect before saving it.

### Remembering Barcode Formats

Barc remembers the last format you entered when adding new barcodes; no more having to pick the format over and over again when adding a bunch of barcodes in a row.

### Halloween Custom Icon

The new "Barc-O-Lantern" app icon is here just in time for spooky season! It's available for all Barc Unleashed subscribers.
