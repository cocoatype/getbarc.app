Barc 25.3 makes a few nice improvements:

### Improved Accessibility

- Barcodes with dates and locations have more-helpful descriptions in VoiceOver.
- The "photo library" toolbar button added in Barc 25.2 is labeled correctly in VoiceOver and Voice Control.

### Brighter Bright Mode

The single-barcode view has a new button to set your screen to beyond maximum brightness, making barcodes easier to scan.

### Easier Imports

Share image attachments from web content such as Mail or Safari into Barc to scan them for barcodes.
